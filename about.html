About

A focused UFC fan mini-site

UFC: Two Per Division distills the sprawling UFC roster into digestible snapshots for quick study and inspiration.

Project blurb

This site highlights the top two contenders in every active UFC division. It was built as an educational exercise in structuring content, managing data-driven pages, and exploring a cohesive dark UI system. Each profile pairs practical stats with narrative context to show why these athletes sit at the top.

Credits & use

  • Fonts: Poppins and Inter via Google Fonts.
  • Icons: lightweight bespoke SVGs inspired by simple geometric line work for clarity on dark backgrounds.
  • Template inspiration: adapted from the Tidy multi-page theme (ThemeWagon) with full restructuring for this project’s scope.
